What a session actually looks like

Nobody sits at a desk.

Your child builds something, cooks something, argues about the rules of a game — then fixes it. That's the afternoon.

Underneath it, they're practising what nobody sits down to teach — starting when they don't feel like it, losing without falling apart, asking for help.

A child who feels tested performs like a child who feels tested. So we don't do that.
