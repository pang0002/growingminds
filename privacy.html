Depending on how you deal with us, we may collect: parent or guardian contact details (name, email, phone); your child's name, age, school, and developmental, learning, health or behavioural information you choose to share; assessment results and progress notes created during our services; job application details and CV files sent through our Careers form; article submissions, including your name and email, sent through our blog submission form; and messages you send us on WhatsApp or email.

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, book and deliver services, assess and support your child, communicate progress to you, administer payments and attendance, review job applications and blog submissions, meet legal obligations, and improve our website and services. Information about your child is shared only with you, our team members who need it to deliver the service, and third parties with your consent (for example, your child's school). We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to address a serious safety concern.

Our services are provided to children under the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian, and we collect information about children from their parents or guardians. This website is intended for parents, guardians and professionals, not for children to use on their own.

We only use photographs or video of children for promotional purposes in line with the media consent choices you make at registration, as set out in our Terms of Service. You can change or withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us.

7. Cookies & Analytics

Google Analytics uses cookies to help us understand how visitors use the site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out browser add-on; the website will still work.

8. Storage, Security & Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including restricting access to team members who need it. We keep records only as long as needed to provide our services, meet legal or professional requirements, and resolve disputes, after which we delete or anonymise them. No online system is completely secure, so please avoid sending highly sensitive details through unsecured channels.
