Supporting children and families

With over eight years of experience working with children and families, Xue Min's approach is shaped by her deep clinical understanding of developmental psychology and early intervention.

The Growing Minds Philosophy

Her core philosophy is simple: every child learns differently, and becoming a capable learner takes more than academics alone. Lasting success comes from nurturing the whole child.

Looking at the Whole Child

At Growing Minds, we help children strengthen the five foundational skills that support all learning:

  • How they think and problem-solve
  • How they learn and process information
  • How they manage their focus and emotions
  • How they build relationships through social skills
  • How they approach challenges with resilience

Because real learning isn’t just about getting the right answers. It’s about developing the confidence, independence, and adaptability to thrive in school, relationships, and the changing world.

"Every child is unique, and no single approach fits every learner. Every child deserves to be understood, supported, and given the opportunity to thrive."

Xue Min Ng
