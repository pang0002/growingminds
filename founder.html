With over eight years of experience working with children and families, Xue Min's approach is shaped by a core philosophy: every child learns differently, and becoming a capable learner takes more than academics alone.

At Growing Minds, she focuses on nurturing the whole child by strengthening the foundational skills that support all learning—how they think, learn, manage themselves, build relationships, and approach challenges.

Because real learning isn’t just about getting the right answers; it’s about developing the confidence, independence, and adaptability to thrive in school, relationships, and the changing world.

"Every child is unique, and no single approach fits every learner. Every child deserves to be understood, supported, and given the opportunity to thrive."

Xue Min Ng
Educational Psychologist
