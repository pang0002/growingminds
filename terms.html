2. Parent or Guardian Consent

Our services are provided to children under the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian. By booking, you confirm you are the child's parent or legal guardian, or have their authority to arrange this on the child's behalf, and that the information you provide is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

3. What Our Services Are — and Aren't

We provide educational-psychology-led assessment and skill-building support. We do not diagnose medical or psychiatric conditions such as ADHD or autism — that sits with paediatricians and doctors. We are not a tutoring service, and we are not a mental health or therapy service. Where a child's needs appear to fall outside our scope, we'll say so and help point you toward the right professional or service.

4. Enrolment & Placement

Programme recommendations are based on your child's needs, background, observation and, where applicable, assessment. For enrolled programmes, the session time reserved is held for your child for the agreed period — the fee secures ongoing placement and practitioner time, not just sessions attended. Activities, goals and support levels may be adjusted as your child progresses; any significant change to programme type, frequency, duration or fee is discussed with you first. We provide services with reasonable professional care and work toward your child's goals, but specific outcomes can't be guaranteed.

5. Programme Fees & Payment

Fees for enrolled programmes are payable in full, in advance, for the entire programme block or term, by the stated due date, unless another schedule is agreed in writing. A registration fee of RM50 applies unless waived (for example, for an existing-client transition). After reasonable written reminders, we may suspend a programme until overdue payment is settled; fees already incurred remain payable. Fee changes are only applied to a future, not-yet-paid block or term — fees confirmed for a current one won't change unless both parties agree.

6. Attendance, Missed & Replacement Sessions

Please notify us as early as possible for any absence. Sessions are generally non-refundable, since your child's slot and practitioner time are reserved regardless of attendance. Replacement policy depends on the programme: our Enrichment Programme does not offer replacement sessions — a missed session is not made up or refunded. Our Individualized Education Programme (IEP) allows up to 2 replacement sessions per term for illness (at least 24 hours' notice where possible) or scheduled medical appointments (around 1–2 weeks' notice where possible), subject to practitioner and timetable availability. For both programmes, sessions falling on public holidays or scheduled closures already factored into the programme calendar are not eligible for replacement or refund. If we need to cancel a session for reasons within our control, we'll arrange a replacement, an alternative practitioner, or a programme credit, so your child doesn't lose a paid session.

7. Withdrawal & Refunds

For enrolled programmes, we ask for at least 4 weeks' written notice (by email or another official channel) to discontinue. Because programme fees are paid upfront to secure your child's placement for the full block or term, fees already paid are non-refundable — whether you withdraw with the required notice, with less notice, or partway through the period. If notice is given before a subsequent, not-yet-paid block or term begins, you won't be charged for it.

8. Health, Safety & Emergencies

Please disclose information relevant to your child's safety and participation — medical conditions, allergies, medication, developmental or behavioural concerns, and any significant changes. A child with a contagious illness, significant fever, or similar shouldn't attend; if a child becomes unwell during a session, we'll contact you or an authorised caregiver for collection. At enrolment, we ask for emergency contact details and your consent for us to take reasonable steps to obtain emergency medical assistance if you and your listed emergency contacts can't be reached promptly — this may include contacting emergency services. Any medical, ambulance or hospital charges from third-party providers remain your responsibility.

9. Confidentiality & Personal Data

We collect and hold information reasonably needed to provide and administer your child's programme, handled in line with applicable Malaysian data protection law and our Privacy Policy. Family information is confidential, and only disclosed where reasonably necessary for administration, with your consent, where legally required, or to address a serious safety concern.

We ask for your separate, explicit opt-in consent for each of the following, and you can say yes to some and no to others — declining any of them doesn't affect your child's enrolment or access to services:

  • Internal documentation — photos or short recordings for internal progress review, supervision or case discussion.
  • Private sharing with you — photos or recordings shared privately to show session updates or progress.
  • Growing Minds social media — identifiable photos, videos or child work on our official social channels.

11. Changes to Programme Arrangement

We may recommend changes to goals, programme type, frequency, duration, group or individual placement, or practitioner. Any change affecting your regular fee or weekly schedule is discussed and confirmed before it takes effect. We aim for continuity, but a specific practitioner or time slot isn't guaranteed indefinitely.

12. Parent–Growing Minds Partnership

We encourage you to share relevant updates, join scheduled reviews, flag significant changes at home or school, and support recommended strategies. With your consent, we may liaise with your child's school, teacher, or other involved professionals.

13. Suspension or Termination by Growing Minds

We may review, suspend or end a programme on reasonable grounds — for example, materially outstanding fees, significant or repeated safety concerns, undisclosed safety-relevant information, the programme no longer suiting your child's needs, or significant risk to your child, others, or staff. We'll generally discuss concerns with you first and give reasonable notice, except where urgent health or safety action is needed. If termination isn't due to unpaid fees or a breach on your part, fees for the period after the termination date are refunded or credited.

16. Limitation of Liability
