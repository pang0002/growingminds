FAQ

Common questions

Is this an IQ test?

Cognitive assessment is usually part of it. You'll get the scores — and, more usefully, what the pattern between them means for your child at school. If you only need a cognitive assessment on its own for a school or an application, tell us and we'll quote for that separately.

Will my child know they're being assessed?

They'll know they're visiting someone who helps children with learning, and we'll help you word it. In the room it's puzzles, games, drawing and talking. Most children enjoy it. Some ask to come back.

Can the school use the report?

Yes. The recommendations are written for teachers as well as parents, and we can join a school meeting if that would help. Many international schools ask for an assessment as part of enrolment. Requirements differ from school to school, so check with yours what they need and tell us when you book.

Do we need a diagnosis first?

No. Many of the children we assess don't have one and never need one.

What if you find something serious?

We'll tell you clearly, in person, without softening it into meaninglessness — and we won't leave you to work out the next step alone.

Not sure if this is the right fit?

Tell us your child's age, what you're noticing, and what you'd like help with. We'll tell you honestly which programme fits — including if the answer is none of them.
