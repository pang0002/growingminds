Discovery & Diagnosis

The Learning Profile

A diagnosis tells you what your child has. A Learning Profile tells you what your child needs.

Most parents arrive knowing something isn't working. Very few arrive knowing why.

A Learning Profile is a structured assessment of how your child learns — attention, memory, reasoning, language, emotional regulation, social understanding and learning behaviour — set against what school and home are actually asking of them. It's carried out by an educational psychologist.

What You Receive

  • 📝 A written Learning Profile, in plain language, that you can keep and share
  • 💪 A clear account of your child's strengths — which matter as much as the difficulties, because they're what any plan gets built on
  • 🏠 Specific recommendations for home — what to change, on which evening, and what to say
  • 🏫 Specific recommendations for school, written so a teacher can act on them
  • 🤝 A feedback session to talk it through, and a channel for the questions that surface a week later

What It Isn't

Not a diagnosis. We don't diagnose ADHD, autism or any other condition — in Malaysia that sits with paediatricians and doctors. If we think you should see one, we'll say so and help you get there.

Not just a score. Cognitive assessment is usually part of it, but a number on its own isn't the deliverable.

Is this an IQ test?

Cognitive assessment is usually part of it. You'll get the scores — and, more usefully, what the pattern between them means for your child at school. If you need a cognitive assessment on its own, we do that too — see Cognitive Assessment.

Can the school use it?

Yes. The recommendations are written for teachers as well as parents, and we can join a school meeting if that would help.
