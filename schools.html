How it works

Three simple steps

1

Tell us what your staff are facing

A short message or call: your school, who the session is for, and what you’d like help with.

2

We shape a session around it

Topic, length, format and audience agreed with you, so it’s built for your team and not off a shelf.

3

We deliver it

Practical, hands-on and grounded in real classroom situations, so teachers leave with strategies they can use straight away.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does a session cost?

Fees depend on the length, format and group size. Tell us what you need and we’ll send you a quote.

How many teachers can attend?

It depends on the topic and the format. Tell us how many staff you have in mind and we’ll shape the session to suit.

Can you tailor the topic to our school?

Yes. Tell us what your staff are dealing with and we’ll shape the session around it.

Do you work with mainstream and special needs schools?

Yes. We’ve delivered sessions for special needs schools and for tuition and enrichment centres, and we work with mainstream classrooms too.

Planning a session for your school?

Tell us who it’s for and what you’d like help with. We’ll come back with a suggested format.
