Our Process

How It Works

01
Assess

We start from a profile

Individual work is planned from an assessment, not a hunch. If your child already has a Learning Profile with us, we start there. If not, we'll usually recommend one — a term of well-aimed work beats two terms of guessing.

02
Goals

We agree goals with you

Three or four, written down, specific enough that you'd know if they'd happened. "Can ask an adult for help without being prompted" — not "improved confidence."

03
Sessions

Sessions

[45–60] minutes, [weekly], one to one. To your child it looks like games, building, drawing, cooking and talking.

04
Review

You get updates that mean something

A short review every [4–6] sessions: what we're seeing, what's moved, what hasn't. If something isn't working we'll say so and change it. You should never be six months in and unsure whether it's helping.

05
Collaborate

We work with school, with your permission

The most useful conversation we have is often the one with the class teacher.

06
Finish

We aim to finish

This isn't meant to be forever. When the goals are met we'll say so — and often the right next step is a club, where the skills get practised with other children rather than with an adult.
