★ Our Philosophy
"I believe every child deserves to be understood before they are supported."

Xue Min has worked with children for over eight years — long enough to see the pattern: a child gets labelled early, everyone works on the behaviour, and nobody asks what's underneath it.

Growing Minds does it the other way round — understand the child first, then decide what helps. She doesn't diagnose. She finds out what's going on and turns it into something you can do.
