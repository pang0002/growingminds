How we work

What we believe

Understand first

We look at how your child learns before we decide what helps. A plan built on a guess is a plan that fails.

Strengths first

We start with what your child can already do. Skills are built on strengths, not on a list of what's wrong.

Learn by doing

Play-based, child-led and practical. Children learn it by doing it, with an adult beside them rather than in front of them.
