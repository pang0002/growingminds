What We Build

The skills behind learning

We translate educational psychology into skills parents can recognise — and work children enjoy doing.

1

Self-Awareness

Recognising strengths, triggers and how they learn best — the starting point for everything else.

2

Self-Management

Handling frustration, staying on task and bouncing back from setbacks without a meltdown.

3

Learning Independence

Planning, organising and checking their own work — so support fades as confidence grows.

4

Thinking Skills

Memory, attention and reasoning — the cognitive engine behind every subject at school.

5

Social Connection

Reading social cues, cooperating and building the friendships that make school feel safe.

Who's Behind Growing Minds

Meet the Founder

Xue Min Ng

Educational Psychologist

"Every child is unique, and no single approach fits every learner. Every child deserves to be understood, supported, and given the opportunity to thrive."

Xue Min has worked with children for over eight years — long enough to see the pattern: a child gets labelled early, everyone works on the behaviour, and nobody asks what's underneath it. Growing Minds does it the other way round — understand the child first, then decide what helps.
