A typical session

What an afternoon looks like

First 10 minutes

Arrival, a warm-up game, a check-in. Every child says one thing before we start. Some weeks that takes thirty seconds. Some weeks it's the most important part of the hour.

Middle 40 minutes

The challenge of the week — building, cooking, a team task, a game with a rule that makes it hard on purpose.

Last 10 minutes

The circle. What was hard, what you tried, what you'd do next time. This is where the afternoon turns into something that comes home with them.

Nobody sits at a desk. Nobody gets corrected in front of everyone. Nobody is compared to anyone else.

Then you get a note

Most classes end when your child walks out of the door. Ours end with a note from us: what your child worked on, what we watched change over the eight weeks, and three things worth keeping going at home. Written by the person who was actually in the room with them.
