Cognitive Assessment

When you need the assessment itself, documented.

Sometimes a school, an application or an examination board asks for a formal cognitive assessment. That's a specific job, and we do it as a standalone service.

What it involves

[1–2] sessions with your child, using standardised instruments administered by an educational psychologist. To your child it looks like puzzles, patterns, questions and talking.

What you receive

A written report covering your child's cognitive profile — reasoning, working memory, processing speed and related abilities — with the scores, what they mean in plain language, and a summary a school can act on.

What it doesn't include

This is the assessment on its own. It doesn't include the wider picture of how your child learns day to day, the classroom and home recommendations, or the ongoing plan — that's a Learning Profile, and for most families it's the more useful of the two. If you're not sure which you need, ask us on the free call. We'll tell you honestly, including when the cheaper one is the right one.
