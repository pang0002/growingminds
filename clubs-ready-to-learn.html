FAQ

Common questions

Does this replace kindergarten?

No. We're here to prepare your child for a smooth start to school — kindergarten is a much bigger, busier environment than home, and children need practice and preparation for that before they're in it every day. Think of this as the run-up, not the replacement.

My child has a diagnosis. Can they join?

Often yes. Tell us at registration and we'll talk it through — we'll either place your child here, or suggest the Early Intervention Programme if that would suit them better.

What if my child cries every morning?

Most do, for the first week or two. Separating is one of the skills we're working on, and we'll agree a plan with you rather than leaving you at the door guessing.

Will you tell me how she's getting on?

Yes — every week. You'll always know how she's doing rather than finding out at the end of a term.

Not sure if this is the right fit?

Tell us your child's age, what you're noticing, and what you'd like help with. We'll tell you honestly which programme fits — including if the answer is none of them.
