How we're different

Not tuition.

Tuition

Teaches children what to learn.

Content, syllabus, practice papers. Useful — and plenty of children need it.

Growing Minds

Teaches children how to learn better.

  • How do I focus when I'm studying?
  • How do I remember what I've learned?
  • What do I do when I'm stuck?
  • How do I prepare for a test?
