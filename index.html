What we're aiming for

Things we'd like your child to be able to do…

Pack their own bag for the right day

Ask an adult for help without being pushed

Lose a game and stay in the room

Start homework without a negotiation

Disagree with a friend and still be friends

Say "I'm stuck on this bit" instead of shutting down

Cope when the plan changes

Finish something boring

None of it is taught at school. All of it can be practised.

Who we work with

Do you need a diagnosis?

No diagnosis is needed. What matters is how your child learns — and helping them move forward, whether they are struggling or simply ready for their next step.

Do you work with children who have ADHD, autism or a learning difficulty?

Yes — and with many who don't. We don't diagnose (that sits with paediatricians), but we'll point you the right way if you need one.

What age range do you work with?

Ages 4–18, across mainstream, inclusive and international schools — many aren't struggling at all, just building skills early.

Understand first

The behaviour you can see is rarely the problem. It's the signal.

Most support in Malaysia works on the behaviour. We work on what's underneath it — which is why the change tends to hold.
