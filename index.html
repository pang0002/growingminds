Why Growing Minds

Every child learns differently

Growing Minds is a Child Development & Learning Studio founded by an Educational Psychologist. We help families understand how their child learns—not just what they can do.

By identifying each child's unique learning profile, we create personalised pathways that strengthen the emotional, social, thinking and learning skills that support lifelong success.

Growing Minds welcomes children of all learning styles and developmental profiles—from those seeking enrichment and school readiness to those who would benefit from additional learning support.

Our goal is simple:

Understand first. Support intentionally. Help every child thrive.

Support Built Around How Your Child Learns

Two ways to get started

Growing Minds provides educational psychology-led support to help you understand your child's learning profile and identify the skills they need to develop. Children can receive support through an individualised programme or join a structured small group — whichever fits your child best.

Understand Your Child

Start with an initial consultation, learning profile and screening or assessment to understand what's really going on — and what to do about it.

Learn About Assessments

Individualised Learning Support

Structured 1-to-1 support for children experiencing difficulties with attention, learning, communication, emotional regulation, social skills or independence — including children with ADHD, autism, learning difficulties or developmental differences, as well as children who simply need a more personalised approach.

Explore Individualised Support

Small Group Programmes

Structured enrichment programmes where children practise emotional, social, thinking and learning skills alongside their peers — grouped by age and developmental level.

View Group Programmes

Parent & School Support

Practical, evidence-informed coaching for parents, plus teacher workshops, classroom consultation and school-based programmes.
