Who we work with

Do you need a diagnosis?

No. Many of the children we see don't have one and never need one. Others arrive with one already and want to know what to actually do with it. What we look at is how your child learns, not what label they carry.

Do you work with children who have ADHD, autism or a learning difficulty?

Yes — and with many children who have none of those.

We don't diagnose; in Malaysia that sits with paediatricians and doctors. If we think you should see one, we'll say so and help you get there.

What age range do you work with?

Children aged [4–18] in mainstream, inclusive and international schools.

Plenty of the children in our clubs aren't struggling at all — their parents simply want them to arrive at eleven able to organise themselves, handle a setback, and speak up for what they need.

What your child actually does

Nobody sits at a desk.

Your child comes to build something, cook something, argue about the rules of a game and get it back on track. That's the afternoon.

Underneath it they're practising the things nobody sits down and teaches — how to start when you don't feel like it, how to lose without it ruining everything, how to ask for help.

A child who feels tested performs like a child who feels tested. So we don't do that.
