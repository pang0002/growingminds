What We Build

The Skills a Classroom Demands

Our Early Intervention Programme focuses on two core areas that are essential for school readiness and social success.

Communication & Play

Building the foundations of social success through joining in, taking turns, sharing, and engaging in back-and-forth interactions with peers.

Classroom Readiness

Developing the crucial skills needed to thrive in a group setting, including sitting for tasks, listening to instructions, and following classroom routines.
