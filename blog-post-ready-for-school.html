Inside a Ready for School session: what the kids actually do

By Growing Minds Team 28 August 2026 4 min read

Walk into a Ready for School Saturday and you'll see kids playing games, not sitting through lessons — but every activity is quietly building a specific readiness skill.

9:00am — Arrival games

Free play as everyone settles in, with facilitators weaving in turn-taking and sharing prompts without making it feel like a lesson.

9:30am — Circle time

Following multi-step instructions, sitting in a group, and waiting for a turn to speak — all foundational classroom skills, practiced through songs and simple routines.

10:15am — Structured activity

A hands-on task (cutting, tracing, building) that builds fine motor control and the ability to follow a task through to completion.

By the time drop-off comes around, most kids don't realise how much they've practiced — which is exactly the point.
