Individualised Learning Support

Support built around how your child learns

Our 1-to-1 programmes support children who may be experiencing difficulties with attention, learning, communication, emotional regulation, social interaction or independence.

We work with children with ADHD, autism (ASD), learning difficulties and developmental differences, as well as children without a formal diagnosis who simply require more structured and personalised support.

Your child may benefit from individualised support if they:

  • Struggle to focus, listen or follow instructions
  • Learn at a different pace from their peers
  • Become easily frustrated or have frequent emotional outbursts
  • Have difficulty expressing their thoughts and needs
  • Struggle with changes, routines or transitions
  • Find it difficult to organise, plan or complete tasks
  • Experience challenges with friendships or social communication
  • Lack confidence or independence when learning

A Personalised Education Plan

Every child receives a Personalised Education Plan based on their strengths, learning needs and developmental priorities. The plan may include goals related to:

  • Attention and learning behaviour
  • Emotional regulation and coping skills
  • Executive functioning
  • Language comprehension and expressive communication
  • Social communication and friendship skills
  • School readiness
  • Confidence and self-awareness
  • Independence and daily routines

How the programme works:

  • 1Understand — initial consultation and review
  • 2Prioritise — identify key skills to develop
  • 3Plan — Personalised Education Plan
  • 4Build — structured 1-to-1 sessions
  • 5Review & generalise — progress tracking

More than academic tuition: our sessions don't focus only on completing schoolwork. We help children develop the underlying emotional, communication, thinking and learning skills they need to participate successfully and become more confident, capable and independent learners.
