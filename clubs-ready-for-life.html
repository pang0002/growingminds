About the programme

Build the skills behind learning

Brain Booster is a learning enrichment programme designed to strengthen the thinking and executive functioning skills children use every day to learn, solve problems and become more independent.

Children are constantly using skills such as attention, memory, self-control, flexible thinking, planning and problem-solving. These skills support how well they listen, follow instructions, organise their ideas, manage challenges and complete tasks.

Through fun, purposeful and hands-on activities, Brain Booster gives children opportunities to exercise these important brain skills while learning strategies they can apply at school and in everyday life.

Is this the right fit?

Who is it for?

Brain Booster is suitable for children who would benefit from developing stronger learning and thinking skills. It can be especially helpful for children who want to:

Improve their attention and concentration

Strengthen their memory

Become more organised

Think more carefully before acting

Improve problem-solving skills

Manage more complex tasks

Become more independent learners

Develop effective learning and study strategies

Brain Booster is an inclusive enrichment programme for children with different strengths and learning profiles. A diagnosis is not required.

Our approach

More than just getting the answer right

At Growing Minds, we are interested not only in whether a child can complete a task, but also in how they approach it.

1

Can they plan before they begin?

2

Can they remember the instructions?

3

Can they change their strategy when something does not work?

4

Can they notice a mistake and correct it?

5

Can they explain what helped them succeed?

By helping children become more aware of these processes, Brain Booster supports the development of more confident, capable and independent learners.

Common questions

Questions parents ask

My child isn't struggling. Is this still for us?

Yes, and plenty of the children here aren't struggling at all. Their parents simply want them to become more organised, more independent, and better at coping when something is hard.

When might a club not be the right fit?

Clubs work because every child can take part with the support a small group can give. We don't provide one-to-one aides inside a club. If your child would need that, tell us when you register — we'll either suggest individual support instead, or talk through whether an adult coming along would make it work.

What ages?

Brain Booster runs in two age bands — 4–6 and 7–9 — grouped separately so the challenges and the conversation are pitched right for each. Tell us your child's age and we'll place them in the right group.

What if we miss a session?

Sessions are non-refundable. It's a small group with a plan that builds week on week, and a spot held for your child affects the group whether or not they're in the room. If you know in advance you'll miss a date, let us know — we'll do what we can, but we can't guarantee a make-up session or a transfer to another block.

Not sure?

Not sure if this is the right fit?

Tell us your child's age, what you're noticing, and what you'd like help with. We'll tell you honestly which programme fits — including if the answer is none of them.
