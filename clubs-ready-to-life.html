Growing up capable.

Secondary school hands your child a lot of responsibility very fast. Their own timetable. Their own homework. Their own decisions about who to sit with and when to ask for help. Most children get that on day one and are expected to work it out. This club gives them eight weeks of practice first.

Come if your child

Still needs you for things they could probably do · won't ask an adult for help, even when they need it · panics when the plan changes · has opinions and won't say them out loud · is heading to secondary and isn't quite ready for how much will be on them.

Eight weeks, eight things to practise

Running your own bag, diary and time. Cooking something real, start to finish. Handling money. Asking a stranger a question. Speaking in front of the group. Disagreeing without falling out. Leading a team, then following one. Fixing a problem when no adult steps in.

What it looks like on a Saturday

Planning and cooking a meal on a budget, including the shopping. Running a session for the younger club. A challenge where we deliberately don't help, and they have to decide together what to do about it.

What you might notice at home

Being asked to do less of it for them.
