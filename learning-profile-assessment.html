Discover Your Child's Unique Way of Learning

Every child learns differently. A Learning Profile provides a comprehensive understanding of how your child thinks, learns, communicates and approaches everyday learning.

Instead of focusing on labels, we focus on understanding your child's unique strengths, developmental profile and learning needs.

A Learning Profile may explore:

  • Learning strengths
  • Communication
  • Language development
  • Executive functioning
  • Attention
  • Emotional regulation
  • Social development
  • Learning behaviours
  • Independence
  • School participation

The journey includes:

  • 1Initial consultation
  • 2Parent discussion
  • 3Standardised assessments (when appropriate)
  • 4Observations (when appropriate)
  • 5Personalised Learning Profile Report
  • 6Feedback session
  • 7Practical recommendations for home and school
