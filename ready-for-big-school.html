The 12-week arc

Three months, three phases

Each month builds on the last — from feelings, to friendships, to full readiness for the classroom.

Month 1 · Me & My Emotions

Understanding feelings, confidence & regulation — like naming big feelings, calming down after a wobble, and celebrating small wins.

Month 2 · Me & Others

Communication, friendships & asking for help — like joining a game, taking turns, and speaking up for what they need.

Month 3 · Me at School

Independence, routines & coping with change — like packing a bag, following a new routine, and handling a change of plan.

A typical session

1
