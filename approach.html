The Science

What Our Work Is Built On

We translate educational psychology into skills parents can recognise — and work children enjoy doing.

🧠

Cognitive Foundations

Working memory, processing speed, attention and reasoning. A bright child can still be drowning — strong reasoning can't compensate for weak working memory.

⚡

Executive Function

The skills that let a child hold instructions, resist distractions, plan ahead, and change approach when the first one fails.

🔄

Self-Regulation & Metacognition

Planning before starting, noticing while working, checking afterwards. Among the highest-impact, lowest-cost things a child can learn.

❤️

Social-Emotional Competence

Self-awareness, self-management, social awareness and relationship skills — the foundations of resilience.

💬

Language & Communication

Chronically under-recognised. A child who "gets angry instead of explaining" is often a child with a language gap nobody has named.
