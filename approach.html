Children get sorted early. Bright or slow, easy or difficult, trying hard or not trying at all.

The labels arrive long before anyone has actually looked at how the child in front of them learns.

We think that's backwards.

It's expensive — in confidence, in wasted years, in children who decide by the age of eight that they're the stupid one. So we start by understanding.
