Calm

Social & Emotions Club

Big feelings. Real friendships.

Some children arrive at school already knowing how to join a game. Some don't, and nobody ever quite explains it to them. This is the club where we explain it. Then we practise it, for eight weeks, in a room where getting it wrong is completely fine.

Come if your child

Falls apart when something goes wrong, and can't tell you why · hovers at the edge of the group, wanting in · can't lose a game without the afternoon ending · has friends, but they never quite stick · goes quiet, or goes loud, and neither one helps.

Eight weeks, eight things to practise

Spotting a feeling before it takes over. Coming back down again. Taking turns when you really don't want to. Walking up to a game that's already started. Disagreeing with someone and still being friends afterwards. Saying sorry and meaning it. Losing. Staying in the room anyway.

What it looks like on a Monday

Cooperative games where one person can't win alone. Stories where somebody gets it badly wrong and has to fix it. Drawing what angry looks like. A team build where two children want different things and have to sort it out themselves — with us close by, not in the middle.

What you might notice at home

A "no" that doesn't collapse the whole evening.
