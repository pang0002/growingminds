The short version

Big feelings, and the friendships they affect

Who It's For

Wants friends and can't quite find the way in — or falls apart when something goes wrong and can't say why.

The Goal

Spotting big feelings early, joining in with confidence, and putting things right when a friendship wobbles.

Our Approach

Four weeks of practice in a room where getting it wrong is completely fine, with us close by, not in the middle.

Common questions

Questions parents ask

My child isn't struggling. Is this still for us?

Yes, and plenty of the children here aren't struggling at all. Their parents simply want them to become more organised, more independent, and better at coping when something is hard.

When might a club not be the right fit?

Clubs work because every child can take part with the support a small group can give. We don't provide one-to-one aides inside a club. If your child would need that, tell us when you register — we'll either suggest individual support instead, or talk through whether an adult coming along would make it work.

What ages?

Friendship Club run in two age bands — 4–6 and 7–9 — grouped separately so the challenges and the conversation are pitched right for each. Tell us your child's age and we'll place them in the right group.

What if we miss a session?

Sessions are non-refundable. It's a small group with a plan that builds week on week, and a spot held for your child affects the group whether or not they're in the room. If you know in advance you'll miss a date, let us know — we'll do what we can, but we can't guarantee a make-up session or a transfer to another block.
