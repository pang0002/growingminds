What happens, step by step

You will not be asked to commit to anything before you understand what you're committing to.

  1. 1
    A free 20-minute call

    You tell us what's worrying you. We tell you honestly whether we're the right people — and if we're not, who is.

  2. 2
    Parent session · 60 minutes

    Just you. Your child's history, what home looks like, what school has said, what you've already tried. We'll ask permission to speak to their teacher, which is usually the most useful conversation we have.

  3. 3
    Sessions with your child

    To your child this feels like puzzles, games, drawing and talking. We use standardised assessments alongside structured observation, choosing tools based on what we're trying to answer rather than running a fixed battery.

  4. 4
    Feedback session and your written profile · 60 minutes

    We go through what we found in plain language. You leave with a written profile you can keep and share. We'll also tell you what we didn't find, which is often the reassuring part.

  5. 5
    Your choice

    Some families take the plan and go. Some want one-to-one work. Some join a club. We'll recommend. You decide.
